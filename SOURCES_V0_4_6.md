# Sources — v0.4.6 Commercial Market Search Profile

Pilot evidence is intentionally restricted to official KONČAR sources.

- KONČAR Group 2025 unaudited Management Board Report: division revenue mix and 2025 export geography.
- KONČAR Group consolidated annual report for 2024: 2024 geography and reported transmission/distribution project examples.
- KONČAR Group 2024 unaudited Management Board Report: country-level order intake.
- KONČAR 2026 half-year results/news release: current Germany, Sweden and Netherlands revenue anchors.
- KONČAR: “A contract for a new transformer substation in Sweden” (11 March 2025): Vattenfall Eldistribution AB, turnkey 130 kV transformer substation.
- KONČAR: “Transformers for Europe's largest agrivoltaic power plant” (17 June 2026): four 125 MVA medium-power transformers for a Brandenburg project connected to a 110 kV network.

Interpretation boundary:
- Revenue geography in the Group report is Group-level unless otherwise stated.
- Division dominance is reported separately from geography.
- Combining the two to define a competitor-search market is analytical (`H1_SEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`), not a reported segment-country fact.
