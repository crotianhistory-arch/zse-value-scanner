# v0.3.1 company-intelligence seed sources

The bundled company profiles are research seeds, not official registry classifications.
Official NACE Rev. 2.1 codes are used as a constrained taxonomy; issuer-to-code mappings are
marked analytical unless an official registry assignment is later retrieved.

## Official taxonomy
- Eurostat — NACE Rev. 2.1 overview and guidance: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nace

## KOEI — KONČAR Group
- KONČAR annual reports page, including audited consolidated Annual Report 2025:
  https://koncar.hr/hr/godisnji-izvjestaji
- KONČAR official H1 2025/2026 reporting is also used as corroboration for the definitions of
  Power Generation, Power Transmission and Distribution, Urban Mobility and Infrastructure,
  and Digital Solutions and Platforms.
- Profile date: 2025-12-31. Segment/product-line values are taken from the audited 2025 Group
  reporting: Power Generation EUR 121.646m; Power Transmission and Distribution EUR 985.606m;
  Urban Mobility and Infrastructure EUR 143.724m; Digital Solutions and Platforms EUR 34.300m;
  Other EUR 34.743m.
