# Sources — v0.4.7 Historical Commercial Trajectory

Pilot evidence is restricted to official KONČAR publications.

- KONČAR Group 2023 consolidated management report: 2022/2023 order intake and 2023 commercial disclosures.
- KONČAR Group sustainability report 2023: rounded Germany/Sweden country export revenue.
- KONČAR Group 2024 financial statements / annual material: Germany/Sweden revenue and 2023/2024 segment comparatives.
- KONČAR 2024 results release: 2024 order intake.
- KONČAR Group H1 2025 Management Board report: H1 2025 Germany/Sweden/Norway revenue, order intake and backlog context.
- KONČAR Group 2025 Management Board report / financial statements: 2025 Germany/Sweden/Norway revenue, 2024/2025 segment comparatives and exact 2024 comparative / 2025 backlog values.
- KONČAR Group H1 2026 results release: H1 2026 Germany/Sweden/Netherlands revenue, group revenue and order intake.

The curated seed prefers explicit reported values and preserves whether a source value is exact or rounded. Where later financial statements provide a comparative figure for the prior year, that comparative can be used for a curated analytical series to improve basis consistency; the source remains attached to the observation and no earlier report is overwritten.
